FAQ

How do I calculate a parlay?
Enter your wager amount, then add legs with their odds. Your payout and win probability update in real time as you type.
How does the hedge calculator work?
Enter your original wager, your parlay's total odds (or potential payout), and the opposing odds on your final leg. The Squeeze calculates exactly how much to bet to guarantee profit regardless of the outcome.
What odds formats are supported?
American (e.g. -110, +200), Decimal (e.g. 1.91, 3.00), and Fractional (e.g. 5/2, 1/1).
Does The Squeeze collect any data?
No. The app works entirely offline and collects zero data. See our privacy policy.
