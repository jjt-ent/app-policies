Privacy Policy

The Squeeze — Parlay & Hedge Bet Calculator

The short version

The Squeeze does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Period.

Data collection

The Squeeze is a fully offline calculator. It does not require an internet connection, user account, or sign-in of any kind. No personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers are collected, transmitted, or stored on any server.

Third-party services

The Squeeze does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

On-device data

Any data you enter into the app (odds, wager amounts, etc.) exists only in the app's active memory on your device and is not persisted or transmitted anywhere. When you close the app, this data is discarded.

Children's privacy

The Squeeze is rated 17+ on the App Store due to its subject matter (sports betting calculations). It is not intended for use by children under 17.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
